# What a compiler does

*Briefly, How It Works · Episode 1 · 1:09 long*

Two computer-generated voices walk through what happens between writing code and running a program: the text is split into tokens, built into a tree, checked, tidied and translated into machine code.

> The voices in this episode are computer-generated (Kokoro text to speech). They are not recordings of real people.
